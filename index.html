Research Interests

My research connects topological data analysis (TDA) and deep learning to study structure in real-world data. I develop topological representations for learning tasks, with applications to impact-echo defect detection and underground pipeline detection in ground-penetrating radar (GPR) data.

My current work focuses on long-range dependencies in neural networks. I use relative homology to design supervisory signals that help models learn spatial relations and connectivity across images. This work includes Satellite PathX, a road-connectivity task developed with collaborators using real satellite imagery and road annotations. More broadly, I am interested in how topology can guide both data representations and learning objectives for practical problems.
